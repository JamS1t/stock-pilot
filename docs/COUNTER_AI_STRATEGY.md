# StockPilot Counter AI - Product Strategy

## Positioning

**StockPilot Counter AI** is a tablet-first, offline-capable AI counter system for serious small retailers.

Plain promise:

> Know what you sold, what stock is missing, who owes you, and what to buy next, even when the internet is bad.

Taglish promise:

> Bantay sa benta, stock, utang, at kita.

## Why This Direction

The product should not compete as another generic POS or a narrow utang tracker.

Generic inventory/POS is crowded. An utang-only app solves a real problem, but it may be too narrow and emotionally sensitive to sell as a standalone monthly product.

The stronger wedge is **counter intelligence**:

- Faster selling at the counter
- Clear daily cash and GCash totals
- Less forgotten utang
- Fewer stockouts
- Easier supplier receipt encoding
- Better reorder decisions
- Clearer profit and margin visibility

Utang remains part of the product, but it is a workflow inside a broader owner operating system.

## Target Market

Start with serious small retailers, not the smallest sari-sari stores.

Ideal early customers:

- Mini-groceries
- Larger sari-sari / sari-sari+ stores
- Neighborhood convenience stores
- School canteens
- Small school-supply stores
- Rice, feed, and dry goods retailers
- Small pharmacies or health-product shops
- Water refilling stations with add-on products
- Small hardware and electrical stores

Ideal first customer profile:

- 100 to 1,500 SKUs
- 50+ transactions per day
- Owner is present but may have helpers or family cashiers
- Uses notebook, memory, Excel, or a basic POS
- Has recurring stockouts, cash mismatch, utang, supplier receipts, or unclear profit
- Can pay PHP 299 to PHP 999 per month if the product saves money

Avoid as the first market:

- Very tiny sari-sari stores with low daily volume
- Stores unwilling to use a tablet, laptop, or counter device
- Stores that only want supplier procurement, because GrowSari and Packworks already cover that direction
- Enterprise retail chains with accounting integrations and custom procurement needs

## Core Pain Points

The product should sell around owner pain, not around software categories.

### Pain 1: "Hindi ko alam totoong kita."

Owners often know cash-in but not real profit after cost, utang, damaged goods, and stock movement.

Product response:

- Daily close
- Expected cash vs actual cash
- GCash total
- Cost and margin tracking
- Weekly owner summary

### Pain 2: "May nawawalang cash."

Cashier mistakes, unpaid items, wrong change, and unrecorded utang create leakage.

Product response:

- Cash session
- Staff PIN
- Payment method tracking
- Adjustment reasons
- Audit log

### Pain 3: "Hindi ko alam ano ang paubos."

Stockouts of fast-moving items lose sales.

Product response:

- Low-stock alerts
- Reorder list
- Sales velocity
- Supplier stock-in tracking

### Pain 4: "Nakakatamad mag-encode ng resibo."

Supplier receipts are tedious to enter manually.

Product response:

- AI receipt scan
- Product matching
- Stock-in draft
- Human confirmation

### Pain 5: "Nakakalimutan ang utang."

Customer credit creates disputes and forgotten balances.

Product response:

- Suki ledger
- Running balance
- Payment history
- Reminder drafts
- Customer statement

## Differentiation

The product should not try to out-GrowSari GrowSari or out-Packworks Packworks.

Differentiate on:

- Counter-first daily operations
- Offline-first reliability
- Tablet/desk mode, not phone-only mode
- AI capture for receipts and voice entry
- Owner decisions, not only reports
- Local workflows: cash, GCash, utang, supplier resibo, family staff

Competitor framing:

- GrowSari: strong in supplier ordering, services, and financing.
- Packworks: strong in sari-sari enablement, data, and broad platform support.
- Generic POS: strong in checkout, weaker in localized small-store workflows.
- StockPilot Counter AI: daily counter operation and owner intelligence.

## Product Pillars

1. **Counter-first**
   The primary screen is a tablet/desk counter dashboard.

2. **Offline-first**
   Selling, utang, payments, and stock movements must work without internet.

3. **AI-assisted, not AI-dependent**
   The app must be valuable manually. AI removes friction after the core workflow works.

4. **Owner-visible money**
   Every feature should connect to lost money, saved time, stock availability, or profit clarity.

5. **Human-confirmed automation**
   AI suggestions should create drafts. The owner confirms before stock, cash, or customer balances change.
