# StockPilot Counter AI - Go-To-Market

## Market Thesis

The first market is not "all sari-sari stores."

The first market is:

> Filipino neighborhood retailers who are too serious for notebooks but too small, offline, and cost-sensitive for enterprise POS.

These stores lose money through stockouts, unclear profit, cash mismatch, slow receipt encoding, and forgotten customer credit.

## Beachhead Segment

Start with serious small retailers:

- Mini-groceries
- Larger sari-sari / sari-sari+ stores
- School canteens
- Small school-supply stores
- Rice/feed retailers
- Small pharmacies or health-product shops
- Water refilling stations with add-on products
- Small hardware/electrical stores

## Buyer Persona

Primary buyer:

- Owner/operator
- 30 to 60 years old
- Present in the store daily
- Has family or staff helping
- Wants control without doing all manual counting
- Comfortable enough with Facebook, GCash, and basic Android

Influencers:

- Cashier/helper
- Owner's child or younger relative
- Accountant/bookkeeper if the store is bigger

## Sales Message

Do not lead with "inventory management."

Lead with:

- "Alam mo agad kung magkano dapat ang cash."
- "Makikita mo kung ano paubos bago maubusan."
- "Picture lang ng resibo, gagawin nating stock-in draft."
- "Hindi na malilimutan ang utang at bayad."
- "Kita mo kung aling item maliit ang tubo."

Short pitch:

> StockPilot is an AI counter system for small stores. It tracks sales, stock, cash, utang, and supplier receipts even offline, then tells the owner what to buy next and where money is leaking.

## Demo Script

Demo should take under 5 minutes.

1. Open tablet counter dashboard.
2. Sell 3 items.
3. Mark one sale as GCash.
4. Add one sale to utang.
5. Record partial bayad.
6. Scan a supplier receipt photo.
7. Show AI stock-in draft.
8. Confirm stock update.
9. Close the day.
10. Show expected cash, low stock, and reorder list.

The demo should prove saved time and clearer money, not feature count.

## Validation Questions

Ask before pitching:

- Paano ninyo sinusulat ang benta araw-araw?
- Paano ninyo alam kung ano ang paubos?
- Kailan ninyo nalalaman kung may kulang sa cash?
- Paano ninyo tina-track ang GCash payments?
- Paano ninyo ina-update ang stock kapag may delivery?
- May utang/listahan ba kayo?
- Ano ang pinaka-nakakainis na part ng pag-manage ng tindahan?
- Magkano ang nawawala kapag nauubusan kayo ng mabentang item?
- Magbabayad ba kayo ng PHP 299 per month kung nasosolve ito?
- Sino ang gagamit sa counter: owner, staff, or family?

## Pilot Plan

Target:

- 10 stores
- 2 to 4 weeks
- Founder-assisted onboarding

Pilot setup:

- Import or create top 50 to 100 products.
- Configure cash/GCash/utang.
- Train owner and one cashier.
- Visit or call twice per week.
- Collect supplier receipt photos for AI eval.

Success metrics:

- 5 stores still active after 14 days.
- 3 stores pay after trial.
- 1 store refers another store.
- 70%+ of store days include at least one transaction.
- 50%+ of active stores complete daily close.
- AI receipt scan is used by at least 3 stores if available.

Kill or pivot signals:

- Owners like the idea but will not enter daily transactions.
- Staff refuses to use it at the counter.
- Setup takes too long for founder-led onboarding.
- Owners only want free tools.
- AI receipt scan is not accurate enough to save time.
- The product does not become part of daily operations within 7 days.

## Onboarding

First 30 minutes must create value.

Minimum setup:

- Store name
- Payment methods
- 20 to 50 top products
- 5 frequent customers if utang is used
- Low-stock thresholds for top sellers
- Opening cash

Do not require full inventory setup before first sale.

Offer fast-start modes:

- Quick POS only
- Inventory-lite
- Utang-lite
- Full stock tracking

## Sales Objections

### "May notebook na kami."

Response:

Notebook records history. StockPilot tells you cash mismatch, low stock, reorder list, and profit leaks.

### "Mahina internet."

Response:

The app is built to work offline at the counter and sync later.

### "Mahirap mag-encode."

Response:

Start with top products only. Receipt scan and quick items reduce encoding over time.

### "Mahal monthly."

Response:

One avoided stockout, one caught cash mismatch, or one corrected price can cover the monthly fee.

### "Hindi techy staff ko."

Response:

Counter screen uses large buttons and simple sale flow. Owner dashboard can stay separate.

## Market Risks

- Very small stores may not pay.
- Setup burden may be high.
- POS behavior is a habit change.
- AI may overpromise if receipt quality is poor.
- Competitors may be stronger in procurement and financing.
- Hardware availability matters.

Risk response:

- Start with serious stores.
- Founder-assisted onboarding.
- Sell offline and daily close before AI.
- Use AI as draft-only.
- Keep pricing affordable but not free.

## What Not To Sell First

- "AI chatbot for your store"
- "Complete ERP"
- "Procurement marketplace"
- "Advanced accounting"
- "Native mobile app"
- "Utang tracker only"

Sell:

> A counter system that helps the owner stop losing money.
